# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenScrcpy is a small open-source project. Everyone is welcome to use it,
report bugs, propose features and contribute code, regardless of experience,
identity, background or language. We want participation to be a respectful and
harassment-free experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep discussions technical and on topic.
- Be patient with people who are new to the project or to Android, Rust,
  Svelte or desktop packaging.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change that does not fit the project,
  and explain why.
- Respect privacy: do not publish someone else's logs, device identifiers or
  personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or sexualised language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer requests to stop a behaviour.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions),
review comments and any other project space. It also applies when someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/yanhenrique-dev). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull
requests, or temporarily or permanently ban a participant, depending on the
seriousness and repetition of the behaviour.

Do not use this channel for security vulnerabilities; follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
